# Supported Sites

The gem reads recipes from **381 popular cooking websites** out of the box. A page of any other
website that publishes schema.org or OpenGraph markup can still be read with
`supported_only: false`, see [Usage](Usage.md#unsupported-sites).

Is a site missing, or has one stopped working?
[Open an issue](https://github.com/davqasd/recipe_scrapers/issues) with the URL of a recipe page,
or [add it yourself](Adding-a-Site.md).
